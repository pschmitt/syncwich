Syncwich is a beautiful, Material You client for your own self-hosted Mealie recipe manager,
built offline-first from the ground up.

Connect it to your Mealie server with a long-lived API token (generated in Mealie itself - your
password is never stored on-device), and Syncwich syncs your recipes, meal plans, shopping lists,
and cookbooks for fully offline browsing. No account, no ads, no tracking - your data stays
between your phone and your own server.

Features:
• Browse and search your recipes, with category and tag filters
• Rich recipe detail: ingredients, steps, images, and nutrition
• View your household's meal plan calendar
• View shopping lists and their items
• Browse cookbooks and their recipe collections
• Everything works fully offline once synced

Syncwich is not affiliated with the Mealie project.
