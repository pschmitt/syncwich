# App icon

## Current: Toast Stack (SW-10, 2026-08-14)

A cartoon, slightly-tilted toast sandwich replaces SW-1's sync-ring + fork glyph, on the same
terracotta background established at launch. Chosen from four concepts (Deli Stack/"Toast Stack",
Club Triangle, Sync Bun, S-Bite) proposed as SVG comps and refined once through a cartoonish/
playful pass per review feedback — the original v1 used a domed kaiser-roll top; v2 switched to
flat toast slices, added thick warm-brown cartoon outlines, tilted each slice slightly off-axis,
and let the lettuce/cheese/tomato bulge past the crust instead of sitting flush.

- **Palette**: `#e4572e` terracotta background (`@color/icon_background`, unchanged from SW-1),
  `#fcefdc` toast, `#8b4a2b` toasted-edge shading, `#6e9c4c` lettuce, `#e4a83a` cheese, `#a83226`
  tomato, `#4a2413` cartoon outline stroke.
- **Foreground** (`syncwich-icon-foreground.svg` → `syncwich-icon-master.png`, 1024×1024,
  transparent): full-color, full-detail glyph — two toast slices tilted a few degrees off-axis
  with rounded cartoon outlines, a lettuce ruffle, a cheese tab drooping past the right edge, and
  two tomato slices poking past the left edge, plus a small gloss highlight on the top slice.
  Positioned in Android's 108dp adaptive-icon safe zone (18dp inset on each side) so it survives
  circle/squircle/rounded-square masking across launchers.
- **Monochrome** (`app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_monochrome.xml`): deliberately
  *simplified*, not a recolor of the foreground — two flat, level bread pills with six small
  filling bumps peeking from the gap between them. The full-color version's tilt and fine detail
  don't hold up as a single-tone themed icon at home-screen scale, and a jaunty rotation reads as
  misaligned once it's stripped of color and tinted by the system, so the monochrome pass flattens
  it back to level and drops the individual lettuce/cheese/tomato shapes down to abstract bumps.
- **Preview** (`syncwich-icon-preview.png`, 1024×1024): the foreground composited over the
  terracotta background, for at-a-glance review — not used by the app itself.
- **Feature graphic** (`syncwich-feature-graphic.svg` →
  `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/featureGraphic.png`, 1024×500, 24-bit/no alpha per Play
  Store's spec): the same glyph at a smaller scale alongside a "Syncwich" wordmark and a one-line
  tagline, for the Play Store listing header.

Source of truth is the SVGs; the PNGs are `magick`-rendered exports (`librsvg` backend) at
`-depth 8`, and `app/src/main/res/mipmap/ic_launcher.png` (pre-API26 legacy fallback, 512×512) is
the preview composite downscaled, matching what `just play-icon-upload` already expects as its
source.

## Previous: sync-ring + fork (SW-1, superseded)

The original launcher icon: a fork glyph inside a two-arrow sync ring, cream-on-terracotta,
generated by a dedicated design pass alongside SW-1's initial app scaffold. Superseded by the
above; no source files were kept for it beyond the monochrome vector's git history.
